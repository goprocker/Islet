About

A small notch for a big screen.

Phones and Macs keep live status in one place at the top. Windows spreads it across the taskbar, the tray and a corner of banners. Islet pulls it into one black pill.

Free and open

MIT licensed. Read the code, change it, ship your own.

Stays on your PC

No account, no cloud, no tracking. Clipboard history never touches disk.

Made for focus

Clicks pass through everywhere except the notch, and it shrinks to a dot when nothing is happening.

Why it exists.

Islet started as a way to see what Claude Code was doing without switching windows. Music came next, then notifications, then the small tools that are always one click too far away.

Every feature has to earn its place in a pill a few centimetres wide. If it can't be read at a glance, it goes in the Dashboard or it doesn't ship.

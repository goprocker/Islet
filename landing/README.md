# Islet landing page

## Sound

No music plays by itself. The notch's play button plays `demo-loop.mp3`, a 32-second cut of the launch film's score, generated by `launch-film/scripts/make_score_apple.py`.

The launch film plays when it scrolls into view. Browsers refuse sound until the visitor's first tap or key press, so before that it plays muted and unmutes on that tap.

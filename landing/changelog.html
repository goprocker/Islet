Changelog

What's new in Islet.

Every release, in plain words. The full history is on GitHub.

2.3.2

Nov 4, 2026Fixes
  • FixedPhone drop download links always give the file you picked, even if the shelf changes.
  • FixedA phone upload that is cut off no longer leaves a half-written file on the PC.
  • FixedThe phone page shows when a send fails, carries on with the other files and lets you retry.
  • FixedImporting a pet from a .zip works again.
  • FixedThe downloads page no longer overlaps its header on phones.

2.3.0

Nov 4, 2026Pets
  • NewLoad your own pet in the Codex Pets format, and the pet now talks in the closed notch.
  • NewThe pet codes along with your AI agent, asks when the agent needs you, and grows a plant after focus sessions.
  • NewThe installer opens the firewall for Phone drop on private networks, and removes the rule on uninstall.
  • FixedIslet now installs for all users, so it asks for admin once during setup.
  • FixedThe pet says different things when idle, not just “Hello”.
  • FixedA background helper that fails to start no longer crashes Islet.
  • FixedQuick notes stop at 20,000 characters instead of silently losing the end.
  • FixedThe notch no longer stops loading after the Search button was removed.

2.2.0

Nov 3, 2026Dock
  • NewA dock with every tool: Phone drop, Links, World clock, Clipboard, Notes, Timer, Shelf, Weather, Units, System and AI coding.
  • NewPhone drop: scan a QR code to move files between your phone and the shelf over Wi-Fi.
  • NewWeather shows the next 6 hours and 5 days, with humidity and wind.
  • NewA Dashboard with Quick Settings tiles, brightness and volume sliders, a greeting and a dock to every tool.
  • NewA pet cat in the notch that reacts to music, focus, pop-ups and battery, and says hello by name.
  • NewUniversal search on Alt+Space, including files and folders by name.
  • NewA Join button for calendar meetings.
  • NewVolume, mute and brightness HUD, and a toast when the microphone is muted.
  • NewPomodoro cycles and a stopwatch; Do Not Disturb turns on during focus.
  • NewClipboard history keeps 20 items with pins and search, plus a quick notes panel.
  • FixedSetting the volume from the dashboard no longer drops it to 0.
  • FixedQuitting no longer touches the window after it is gone.

2.1.0

Nov 2, 2026Launch
  • NewA welcome screen that asks before changing anything on first run.
  • NewBackground auto-update from GitHub Releases.
  • NewThe waveform follows audio from whichever media player is shown.
  • NewThe Islet logo in the app, website and README.
  • FixedSeek and volume controls work from the keyboard.
  • FixedThe notch moves to the right place when display scaling changes.
  • SecurityAgent hook events are authenticated and the hook secret is locked down.

2.0.0

Oct 30, 2026Hardening
  • FixedCalendar honours time zones and daylight saving, and stops guessing rules it can't read.
  • FixedLyrics handle multiple timestamps and come back after the notch reloads.
  • FixedQueued pop-ups wait while the notch is hidden.
  • FixedSettings keep edits made while an autosave is running.
  • FixedSimultaneous downloads match the right files.

1.0.0

Oct 29, 2026First release
  • NewMusic, Claude Code sessions, notifications and battery in one notch.
  • NewFile shelf, clipboard history, weather and calendar.
  • NewA black-hole animation when the charger goes in or out.
